# Help With VA Form 22-1990n — NCS Education Benefits

Find answers to common questions about this application. If you need more help, contact us using the information at the bottom of this page.

[Start your application](/education/apply-for-education-benefits/application/1990n)

---

## About the NCS education benefit

### What is the National Call to Service education benefit?

The National Call to Service (NCS) program is a DoD enlistment incentive program. If you enlisted under NCS and chose one of the two NCS education incentives on DD Form 2863 (NCS Election of Options), you may be eligible for a VA education benefit under Section 510 of Title 10, U.S. Code.

This benefit is separate from the Post-9/11 GI Bill (Chapter 33) and other VA education programs.

### Who is eligible for this benefit?

You may be eligible if you:

- First entered military service on or after October 1, 2003
- Signed a DoD enlistment contract under the National Call to Service program
- Chose one of the two NCS education incentives on DD Form 2863

### What types of training does this benefit cover?

This benefit may cover:

- College or other school (including online courses)
- Apprenticeship or on-the-job training
- Vocational flight training
- Correspondence courses
- National test reimbursement (such as SAT or CLEP exams)
- Licensing or certification test reimbursement (such as EMT, NCLEX, or MCSE exams)

## About the application

### How do I apply?

You can apply online at [va.gov/education/apply-for-education-benefits/application/1990n](/education/apply-for-education-benefits/application/1990n).

You can also apply by paper. [Download VA Form 22-1990n (PDF)](/find-forms/about-form-22-1990n), complete it, and mail it to the correct VA Regional Processing Office based on where your school is located (or your home address if you haven't chosen a school yet).

### Do I need to sign in to apply?

You don't have to sign in, but we recommend it. When you sign in, we'll fill in some of your personal information — like your name, address, and contact details — from your VA records. This saves time and reduces errors.

### How long does the application take?

Most applicants complete this form in about 15 minutes if they have all their documents ready. Your progress saves automatically. You can return to finish your application within 60 days.

### Can I save my application and come back to it?

Yes. If you're signed in, your application saves automatically after each section. You can return to it at any time within 60 days from the [My VA page](/my-va) on VA.gov.

## About required documents

### What is DD Form 2863, and where do I get it?

DD Form 2863 (NCS Election of Options) is the form you signed when you chose your NCS incentive during enlistment. This document is required. We cannot process your application without it.

If you can't find your copy, contact your branch of service's personnel records office or the National Personnel Records Center (NPRC):

- **Phone:** 1-314-801-0800
- **Website:** [archives.gov/personnel-records-center](https://www.archives.gov/personnel-records-center)

### What is the DD Form 214 Member 4 copy?

Your DD Form 214 is your Certificate of Release or Discharge from Active Duty. The form has multiple copies. We require the **Member 4 copy** (also called the "Member Copy 4"). This is the copy you received at discharge. Other copies are not accepted.

If you can't find your Member 4 copy, you can request it through the National Personnel Records Center (NPRC) or milConnect at [milConnect.dmdc.osd.mil](https://milconnect.dmdc.osd.mil).

### What if I'm on terminal leave and don't have my DD Form 214 yet?

You can still submit your application. Upload your DD Form 2863 now. When your DD Form 214 (Member 4 copy) is issued after your discharge, mail it to your VA Regional Processing Office.

### What file types and sizes are accepted for document uploads?

Accepted file types: PDF, JPG, PNG.
Maximum file size: 20 MB per file.

## About specific sections of the application

### I'm applying for vocational flight training. What are the requirements?

To use this benefit for vocational flight training, you must already hold a private pilot's license.

You also need a valid medical certificate:
- **Airline Transport Pilot (ATP) course:** You need a valid **first-class** medical certificate on the date you enter training.
- **All other flight courses:** You need a valid **second-class** medical certificate on the date you enter training.

You'll be asked to confirm that you hold the required certificates during the application.

### I'm on active duty. Is there anything special I need to do?

Yes. If you're currently on active duty, you must consult with an Education Service Officer (ESO) before you submit this application. You'll certify that you've done this on the review page before submitting.

### What is a Senior ROTC scholarship program?

A Senior ROTC scholarship under Section 2107 of Title 10, U.S. Code provides funds that pay for tuition, fees, books, and supplies at a college. The application asks whether you participate in this program because it may affect your benefit calculation.

### What is Federal Tuition Assistance, and does it affect my NCS benefit?

Federal Tuition Assistance (FTA) is funding from the Armed Forces or Public Health Service that pays for education courses. The application asks active duty servicemembers whether they're receiving or expect to receive FTA or similar funding. Answering "Yes" does not disqualify you from the NCS benefit, but it affects how VA calculates your payment.

### Why does VA need my bank account information?

VA pays most education benefits by direct deposit to your bank account. You'll need to provide your routing number, account number, and account type (checking or savings), and upload a voided check or deposit slip.

**Note:** Direct deposit is not available for Chapter 32 recipients.

If you don't have a bank account, the Veterans Benefits Banking Program (VBBP) can help you find one. Visit [benefits.va.gov/benefits/banking.asp](https://benefits.va.gov/benefits/banking.asp) or call 1-800-827-1000.

If you choose not to enroll in direct deposit, contact the Department of the Treasury at 1-888-224-2950 to request a waiver.

## After you apply

### How long does it take to process my application?

VA aims to process education benefit applications within 30 days of receiving a complete application with all required documents.

### How do I check my application status?

After you submit your application, you'll receive a confirmation page with a claim reference number. You can track your claim status at [va.gov/claim-or-appeal-status](/claim-or-appeal-status) or by calling the GI Bill Hotline.

### What is a Certificate of Eligibility (COE)?

A Certificate of Eligibility (COE) is the official document VA sends you when your application is approved. You give this document to your school's Veterans Certifying Official (VCO). The VCO then certifies your enrollment with VA so you can receive your benefit payments.

### What if my application is missing documents?

If VA needs more information or documents to process your application, a VA representative will contact you. To avoid delays, make sure you upload both your DD Form 2863 and your DD Form 214 (Member 4 copy) before you submit.
