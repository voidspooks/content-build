# Application for VA Education Benefits Under the National Call to Service (NCS) Program

Use this form to apply for your education benefit if you enlisted under the National Call to Service (NCS) program. This benefit is under Section 510 of Title 10, U.S. Code.

VA uses this application to decide if you qualify for NCS education benefits and to set up your payments. This is the online version of VA Form 22-1990n.

[Start your application](/education/apply-for-education-benefits/application/1990n/applicant-information/personal-information){: .usa-button-primary }

## Check if you qualify

You may qualify if all of these are true:

- You first entered military service on or after October 1, 2003.
- You signed an enlistment contract with the Department of Defense under the National Call to Service program.
- You chose one of the 2 education incentives on your DD Form 2863, NCS Election of Options.

If you chose a cash bonus instead of an education incentive, you do not qualify for this benefit.

## What you'll need

Have these documents ready to upload:

- **DD Form 2863, NCS Election of Options.** This is required.
- **DD Form 214, Member 4 copy.** This is required. If you are on terminal leave, you can submit your application now and upload this form when it's issued.
- **A voided personal check or a deposit slip.** You need this if you want direct deposit.

You also need:

- Your Social Security number
- Your bank account and routing numbers, if you want direct deposit
- The name and address of your school or training place, if you know them
- Your service dates

If you're on active duty, talk with an Education Service Officer (ESO) about your education program before you submit. You will need to certify that you did this.

## How long it takes

Plan for about 15 minutes to complete this form. Your progress saves as you go. You can return within 60 days to finish.

## Before you submit

Your application is not complete until you submit it. After you submit, you'll get a confirmation with a claim reference number.
