# Apply for VA Education Benefits Under the National Call to Service Program

VA Form 22-1990n | OMB Control No. 2900-0154 | Expires March 31, 2026

Use this application if you enlisted under the National Call to Service (NCS) program and elected an education incentive. This benefit is separate from the GI Bill and other VA education benefits.

[Sign in to start your application](/education/apply-for-education-benefits/application/1990n)

Signing in lets us fill in some of your information for you from your VA records.

---

## Am I eligible for this benefit?

You may be eligible if you meet **all three** of these conditions:

1. **You first entered military service on or after October 1, 2003.**
2. **You signed an enlistment contract with the Department of Defense (DoD) under the National Call to Service program.**
3. **You chose one of the two NCS education incentives** as documented on DD Form 2863 (NCS Election of Options).

> **Not sure if you qualify?** Call the GI Bill Hotline at [1-888-GI-BILL-1](tel:+18884424551) (1-888-442-4551), Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. ET. TTY: 711.

---

## What documents do I need?

Gather these documents before you start. You'll need to upload them as part of your application.

**Required documents:**

- **DD Form 2863 (NCS Election of Options)** — This is the form you signed when you chose your NCS incentive. This document is required. We can't process your application without it.
- **DD Form 214 (Member 4 copy)** — This is your Certificate of Release or Discharge from Active Duty. Make sure you upload the Member 4 copy, not other copies.

> **On terminal leave?** You can submit your application now and send your DD Form 214 (Member 4) to VA when it's issued.

**Required if you're enrolling in direct deposit:**

- A voided personal check or deposit slip showing your routing number and account number.

**Active duty servicemembers:**

- You must consult with an Education Service Officer (ESO) before you submit this application.

---

## What information will I need?

You'll need the following information to complete this application:

- Your Social Security number
- Your date of birth
- Your contact information (address, phone number, email address)
- Your military service dates, service component, and service status
- The name and address of the school or training program you plan to attend (if you've already selected one)
- Your educational or career goal
- Your bank account information (if you want direct deposit payments)

---

## How long will this take?

Most applicants complete this form in about **15 minutes** if they have all their documents ready.

Your application will be saved automatically as you go. You can return to finish it within 60 days.

---

## What happens after I apply?

After you submit your application:

1. **VA reviews your application.** We'll check your eligibility and the documents you submitted.
2. **VA sends you a Certificate of Eligibility (COE)** if you're approved.
3. **Give your COE to your school's Veterans Certifying Official.** They'll certify your enrollment with VA using VA Form 22-1999.
4. **VA pays your benefit** based on your enrollment certification.

---

## Steps to apply

1. Sign in to your VA.gov account or create one.
2. Complete all sections of the application.
3. Upload DD Form 2863 and DD Form 214 (Member 4 copy).
4. Review your information and submit.

[Sign in to start your application](/education/apply-for-education-benefits/application/1990n)

If you prefer to apply by paper, you can [download VA Form 22-1990n (PDF)](/find-forms/about-form-22-1990n).
